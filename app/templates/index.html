A collaborative catalog of vendors, products, and platform identifiers. Explore CPE records and package URL (PURL) mappings, connect related products, and help improve the data through reviewed proposals.

Common Platform Enumeration (CPE) gives software, hardware, and operating systems consistent names so teams can match platforms to vulnerability records.

Search the catalog Contribute an update

Part of the GCVE initiative

An open approach to vulnerability identification.

GCVE, the Global CVE Allocation System, supports decentralized vulnerability identification and publication. CPE Editor contributes a curated platform catalog to that open ecosystem.
